CRITICAL INVARIANTS — DO NOT REMOVE
- Opening retirement freezes new commitment intake on the target component.
- Plan definition pins target hash, optional replacement hash, and snapshot commitment count.
- Only the component owner can register commitments; arbitrary callers cannot grief retirement with spam obligations.
- The issuer cannot unilaterally declare satisfaction final; beneficiary confirmation is required.
- Only the beneficiary can release an obligation.
- Semantic assessment has exactly four settlement classes: TARGET_REQUIRED, SUBSTITUTE_OK, NO_TARGET_DEPENDENCY, AMBIGUOUS.
- Validators must independently re-run the semantic classification; do not replace the custom validator with format-only validation.
- AMBIGUOUS fails closed.
- SUBSTITUTE_OK is only eligibility. It does NOT clear the old obligation until the beneficiary explicitly rebinds it.
- Automatic v1 rebind is restricted to a same-owner active replacement component.
- Rebind creates a child commitment on the replacement and terminally marks the old commitment REBOUND; the obligation must not disappear.
- Finalization requires zero unresolved commitments.
- `is_quiescent` is bound to the exact certified plan definition hash.
- No owner/admin emergency bypass may force retirement over unresolved obligations.
